About Maria

Maria Orantes is a bilingual REALTOR® based in Woodstock, GA, working with Maximum One Executive Realtors out of the Alpharetta office. Before real estate, she spent more than twenty years working closely with families navigating important, often confusing legal decisions — experience that now shapes how she guides clients through buying and selling a home.


She works with buyers, sellers, and first-time homeowners throughout North Georgia, with particular care for Spanish-speaking families who want every step of the process explained clearly — not just translated.
